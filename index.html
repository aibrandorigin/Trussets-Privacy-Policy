This Privacy Policy explains how Trussets collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Trussets mobile application, website and related services (together, the “Services”). Trussets is a property marketplace intended to help property owners connect directly with prospective buyers and tenants. By using the Services, you acknowledge this Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and contact information: name, mobile number, email address, account credentials and authentication or verification information, such as one-time passcodes (OTPs).
  • Property listing information: property address or locality, type, description, price or rent, photographs, facilities and other details you choose to provide.
  • Property and identity documents: documents you upload for a listing or verification, which may include sale deeds, encumbrance certificates, link documents, property-tax receipts, no-objection certificates and, where you choose to submit them, identity documents such as Aadhaar, PAN or passport.
  • Communications and support information: enquiries, reports, messages and information you provide when contacting our support team.
  • Transaction and access information: records relating to plans, purchases, payments, refunds or access to paid features. Payment card or bank credentials should be handled by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Trussets unless expressly stated at checkout.
  • Device, usage and technical information: IP address, device and browser information, app version, log data, approximate usage events and diagnostic information needed to operate and secure the Services.
  • Location information: device location may be accessed if you grant the relevant permission and use a feature that needs it. You can manage location permission in your device settings; some location-based features may not work without it.

We collect information you provide, information generated when you use the Services and information received from service providers that help us operate authentication, hosting, communications and payments.

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage accounts, authenticate users and send OTPs or important service messages.
  • Publish and manage property listings, respond to enquiries and connect owners with prospective buyers or tenants.
  • Review submitted property or identity documents and administer verification indicators or badges.
  • Provide paid plans, process transactions, maintain purchase records and resolve billing disputes.
  • Provide customer support, handle complaints and prevent fraud, misuse and security incidents.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, analyse and improve the performance and safety of the Services.
  • Comply with applicable laws and enforce our terms and policies.

3. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as described below and as needed to provide the Services:

  • With marketplace users: listing details and information you choose to make public are visible to users of the marketplace. Depending on the feature and access purchased, a prospective buyer or tenant may receive owner contact details and/or access to property documents. Do not upload information you are not authorised to disclose. Review the listing and access settings before publishing.
  • With service providers: we use third-party providers for infrastructure, database and file storage, authentication, OTP/SMS delivery, payment processing, security and technical support. These providers may process information on our behalf under their own terms and applicable safeguards. The app implementation uses Google Firebase services and Google Cloud infrastructure; the SMS gateway and payment provider may vary by configuration.
  • For legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, protect users or the public, investigate fraud, or protect our rights and Services.
  • Business changes: information may be transferred as part of a merger, restructuring or sale of the relevant business, subject to appropriate protections.

4. Property documents and verification

Documents submitted for a property listing or verification are used to review the listing, support verification and provide the document-access features described in the app. Certain documents may be made available to a buyer or other eligible user after the applicable owner-access or purchase step. Verification is an internal platform process and should not be treated as a government certification, legal opinion or guarantee of title. Please redact unrelated sensitive information before uploading where possible.

5. Permissions and files

The Android app may request internet and network access, camera or photo/media access for property images and documents, and location access for location-related features. You can deny or revoke optional permissions in Android settings. The app may not be able to provide the related feature if permission is denied. Files and documents you choose to upload are transmitted to our systems and/or storage providers so they can be used for the feature you selected.

6. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and its purpose. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details, subject to lawful retention requirements and backup cycles.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You may review or update certain account and listing information through the app. You may manage device permissions through Android settings and may request access, correction or deletion of personal information by contacting us. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Deleting information may affect your ability to use some or all Services. We may retain information where required or permitted by law, to complete transactions, resolve disputes or prevent fraud.

8. Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your Trussets account and associated personal information, email support@trussets.in from your registered email address or include your registered mobile number and ask for “Account and Data Deletion”. We may contact you to verify the request. We will process eligible deletion requests within the period required by applicable law. Certain transaction, security, legal or dispute-related records may need to be retained for a limited period.

9. Children’s privacy

The Services are intended for adults involved in property-related activities and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in locations outside your state or country. Where this occurs, we take steps intended to ensure that the processing is subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable law.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when our Services, practices or legal obligations change. We will publish the revised version on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or other appropriate channels.
